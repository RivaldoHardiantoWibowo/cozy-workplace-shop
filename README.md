# The Modern Workspace

VISUAL DESIGN PROMPT: Modern Office Furniture E-commerce Website

Paste this entire prompt into your AI design/coding tool as the visual/UI brief. This is purely about look, feel, and layout — no backend, platform, or data logic included.

1. Overall Aesthetic & Brand Mood

Design a clean, premium, approachable e-commerce website for office furniture (chairs, desks, accessories). The mood should feel:

Warm minimalism — plenty of whitespace, but not cold or sterile

Trustworthy and modern — like a DTC (direct-to-consumer) brand, not a corporate B2B catalog

Comfort-forward — imagery and copy should evoke "a better way to work," not just "furniture for sale"

Confident, not flashy — no gradients, no heavy shadows, no skeuomorphism. Flat, considered, editorial

Think: the visual language of a well-funded DTC startup — somewhere between a lifestyle brand and a functional tool store.

2. Color Palette

Base: off-white / warm cream background (avoid pure white, avoid pure gray)

Primary text: near-black or deep charcoal, not pure black

Accent color: one confident brand color (pick something warm — terracotta, deep green, or navy — avoid generic e-commerce blue unless it's a specific rich navy)

Secondary accent: a soft muted tone for badges/tags (sand, sage, dusty blue)

Sale/promo color: warm red or orange, used sparingly (only for actual discounts, not everywhere)

Product photography should sit on neutral light backgrounds so colorful accent chips (used in swatches) pop against it

3. Typography

Headings: a clean, slightly rounded or geometric sans-serif with confident weight (medium-bold) — should feel modern and friendly, not corporate

Body text: a highly readable sans-serif, generous line-height (1.5–1.6), medium gray/charcoal color

Hierarchy: use size and weight, not color, to establish hierarchy. Avoid more than 3 font sizes per section

Price should always be visually prominent — bold, slightly larger than surrounding text

Avoid all-caps for long text; reserve all-caps (small, letter-spaced) for labels, badges, and section eyebrows only

4. Layout & Spacing Principles

Generous whitespace between sections — sections should breathe, not feel stacked

12-column grid, max content width around 1280–1440px, centered

Section padding: large vertical spacing (80–120px desktop, 48–64px mobile) between major homepage blocks

Cards and grids: consistent gutter spacing, rounded corners (8–12px radius), no harsh borders — use subtle shadows or just whitespace to separate

Avoid visual clutter: each section should have ONE clear focal point, not five competing CTAs

5. Imagery & Photography Style

Product shots: clean studio photography on neutral/light backgrounds, product centered, consistent angle across the catalog

Lifestyle shots: real-feeling home/office environments, natural light, candid rather than overly staged

User-generated content style: slightly less polished, phone-camera aesthetic, mixed into an otherwise polished site to build authenticity/trust

Use image aspect ratio consistency within each grid (e.g., all product cards use the same ratio)

Hero section: large, high-quality lifestyle image or subtle looping video showing the product in real use

6. Component Styling

Buttons

Primary CTA: solid fill using the accent color, rounded corners (6–8px), medium padding, no gradient

Secondary CTA: outline or ghost style, same corner radius

Hover state: subtle darken/lighten, no dramatic scale or shadow pop

Product Cards

Image on top (square or 4:5 ratio), name, price, rating stars + review count below

Variant swatches shown as small circular or rounded-square color chips directly on the card

A quiet "quick add" button that appears on hover (desktop) or is always visible (mobile)

Small badge in a corner for "New," "Limited," or "Sale" — muted background, not neon

Variant Swatches (color/material selectors)

Circular swatches for colors, showing actual color/material thumbnail

Rounded rectangle buttons for text-based options (size, style)

Selected state: a ring/border around the swatch, not a full color change

Out-of-stock or limited variants: visually muted (lower opacity) with a small text label

Badges & Tags

Small, pill-shaped, low-saturation background colors

Used for: New, Limited, Sale, Bestseller — one badge per card maximum to avoid clutter

Rating Display

Filled/outline star icons, small, paired with numeric rating and review count in muted gray text

Clicking the rating scrolls to the reviews section

Forms & Inputs

Simple bordered inputs, rounded corners matching buttons, generous padding, clear focus state (accent-colored outline)

7. Section-by-Section Visual Breakdown

Header (sticky)

Slim promotional bar at the very top, low-contrast background, centered text, dismissible

Below it: logo left, primary nav center or left-aligned, cart + account icons right

Mega-menu dropdown: two-column layout, small thumbnail images next to category links

Search bar expands from an icon into a full-width overlay/dropdown with suggested searches

Hero (homepage)

Full-width or near-full-width image/video background

Large headline (short, benefit-driven), one-line subtext, two CTA buttons (primary + secondary/ghost)

Optional small badge/eyebrow text above the headline (e.g., a seasonal callout)

Value Proposition Strip

4-column grid (2-column on mobile), each with a small line-icon, short bold label, one-line description

No borders between columns — just spacing

Curated Collections Grid ("shop by lifestyle/persona")

Grid of square or portrait image cards, each with an overlay label

Optional filter tabs above the grid to switch between collection groupings

Hover state: subtle image zoom or overlay darken

"Shop the Room" / Scenario Gallery

Large lifestyle photo with numbered circular hotspots overlaid on top of specific products in the image

Clicking a hotspot opens a small popover/card with product thumbnail, name, price, and a link

Product Listing Page (PLP)

Left sidebar filters (checkboxes for category, color, material) on desktop; filter drawer on mobile

Top bar: result count + sort dropdown, right-aligned

Responsive grid: 4 columns desktop, 2 columns tablet, 1–2 columns mobile

Below the grid: a horizontal comparison table (sticky header row, columns = products, rows = attributes) for products within that category

Bundle cards further down: two product thumbnails side by side with a "+" between them, combined price with strikethrough original price

Product Detail Page (PDP)

Two-column layout desktop: image gallery (left, ~55–60% width) + product info (right, sticky while scrolling)

Image gallery: large main image, thumbnail strip below or to the side, swaps based on selected variant

Info column, top to bottom: title, rating, price, quick specs line, variant swatches (grouped by attribute, clearly labeled), quantity selector, add-to-cart button (full width, prominent), accessories/add-on checkboxes below the button, bundle-and-save module

Tabs below the fold: Overview / Specs & Dimensions / Materials

Feature highlight grid: icon + short image + short caption, 2–3 per row

Reviews section: overall rating breakdown (horizontal bar chart per star level), filterable by keyword tags, sortable, review cards with reviewer name, verified badge, star rating, short review text

Cart Drawer

Slides in from the right, dims the background

Top: "Your Cart" heading + close icon

Free shipping progress bar directly below the heading — a thin horizontal bar that fills as the cart total approaches the free-shipping threshold, with a short encouraging label

List of cart items: thumbnail, name, selected variant, quantity stepper, price, remove icon

Empty state: friendly illustration/icon + "Start Shopping" button

Below items: a small horizontal scroll of recommended add-on products

Sticky bottom bar: subtotal + full-width checkout button

Footer

Multi-column link layout (4–5 columns): Shop, Company, Support, Legal

Newsletter signup: simple email input + button, one short sentence of copy above it

Social icons row

Small print: copyright, address if relevant

8. Micro-interactions & Motion

Keep animations subtle and fast (150–250ms transitions)

Image swaps on variant selection: instant or quick crossfade, no jarring jump

Cart drawer: smooth slide-in/out, background dim fades in parallel

Hover states on cards: gentle image scale (1.02–1.05x) or soft shadow lift, nothing bouncy

Scroll-triggered fade-ins for homepage sections are optional and should be minimal — don't delay content visibility

9. Responsive Behavior

Mobile-first: single-column stacking for all multi-column sections

Sticky "Add to Cart" bar should appear at the bottom of the screen on mobile PDP once the user scrolls past the main button

Filters on PLP collapse into a full-screen or bottom-sheet drawer on mobile

Mega-menu becomes an accordion-style expandable menu on mobile

10. What to Avoid

No stock-photo-looking generic office imagery (people in suits shaking hands, etc.)

No overly saturated or neon colors

No dense, cluttered grids with too many competing CTAs per screen

No heavy drop shadows, no skeuomorphic 3D buttons

No more than one animated/motion element visible at a time in any given viewport
